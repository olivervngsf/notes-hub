# Idea capture via chat, not an app

## Idea
Random ideas pop into my head — work, personal, whatever. I don't want to open an app or write anything myself. I want to just talk to Claude, have it figure out what I mean, break it down, and save it as a short markdown note.

## Translation
You don't want a notes app, you want a friend who writes things down for you. Talk first, structure later — Claude listens, guesses what kind of idea it is, restates it in plain words, and turns it into 2-5 doable steps. No blank page, no typing.
