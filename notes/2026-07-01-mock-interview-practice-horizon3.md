# Practice mock interviews (Horizon3 scenarios)

## Idea
This month's focus: practice mock interviews. Specifically want to break down and practice scenarios from the last interview with Horizon3's hiring manager.

## Translation
You have a real, recent interview to learn from instead of generic prep — the plan is to pull apart what actually happened with Horizon3's hiring manager and turn it into practice scenarios, so next time you're sharper on that exact kind of conversation.

## Questions to Practice
1. **Fast onboarding + parallel execution** — New domain, startup speed: how do you ramp up product expertise *and* ship small wins at the same time? What's your technique?
2. **Alignment workshop** — High-visibility project, small tiger team (design/eng/PM), CEO watching: what do you run early (workshops, etc.) to get shared understanding fast and avoid design rework later?
3. **Handling engineering pushback** — Design is locked, ready to hand off — engineering comes back saying it's too ambitious for the timeline they committed to. How do you react and resolve it?
4. **AI in the design process** — Real experience prototyping with AI tools (Claude Code, etc.) or building skills to speed up design. How does it reshape the traditional design process?
5. **Career arc** — A few years out: individual-contributor craft, or design leadership? What does the ideal future look like?
