# Strategy to move forward after manager talk

## Idea
After talking with my manager, I keep thinking about a strategy for what's next — how to actually move forward from that conversation.

## Translation
The conversation opened a door but you haven't decided what to walk through yet. This needs you to land on what "moving forward" means before you can act.

## Next Actions
- [ ] Write down the key takeaways from that conversation
- [ ] Decide what "moving forward" means (promotion? new project? skill gap?)
- [ ] Pick one first step
